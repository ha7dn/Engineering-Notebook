For the past few days I've been studying and learning about the Single Responsibility Principle (SRP), about invariants, aggregates and Domain Driven Design. The last two subject I came across because of a question asked to ChatGPT, which I'm using as a mentor alongside my own research. The answer was about how to separate a muddied service to make the separation of concerns cleaner, specifically the validation part.
It took me some days to understand what invariants are, how the work and what their purpose is, and it was the same with aggregates.
The way I understand it, invariants are the business rules that must always be true in a domain, there's different ways to apply and validate them (factory methods, encapsulation) and our entities are the smallest unit where these business rules live. Aggregates are groups of related entities that in DDD are a consistency boundary. What this means is that when modifying these elements, they have to be changed in the same transaction (Eg: you cannot modify items of an order without modifying the order itself).
