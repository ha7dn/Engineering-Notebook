What does "Now listening on: http://[::]:8080" mean?
- means: your application is telling you that it is listening on TCP port 8080 on all IPv6 addresses it owns.

the  ports 8080 and 8081 are the internal ports listening INSIDE the container. To do a port forwarding, specify in the compose.yaml
```
    ports:
      - "5000:8080"
      - "5001:8081"
```
where 5000 is the http port from the application's lauchSettings, and 5001 is the https port.
